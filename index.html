Bible, Hermeneutics & Theology

Build deep biblical foundations

Whole-Bible Track

Read all 66 books, then create one-page outlines and teach 10-minute overviews of every book.

Hermeneutics

Study genre, historical context, literary context, authorial intent, and canonical context.

Preaching & Teaching

Develop sermon competence

Sermon Lab

Analyze sermons, prepare short expositions, and preach full messages across multiple biblical genres.


Pastoral Care

Learn to shepherd people

Study 1 Timothy 3, Titus 1, 1 Peter 5, crisis referral, safeguarding, conflict, visitation, funerals, weddings, prayer ministry, and pastoral counseling boundaries.

Leadership & Governance

Lead with biblical structure and accountability

Study elders, deacons, meetings, finance, stewardship, conflict, discipline, delegation, volunteer leadership, and administration.

Holy Spirit & Charismatic Ministry

Practice Spirit-empowered ministry with order

Study Acts 2, 8, 10, 19; Romans 12; 1 Corinthians 12–14; Ephesians 4; 1 Thessalonians 5. Develop biblical guidelines for prophecy, tongues, healing prayer, discernment, correction, and oversight.

Church Planting

Prepare for Awaken Life Church

Delay complexity. Build calling, character, doctrine, pastoral competence, leadership experience, community exegesis, financial runway, and launch-team readiness before planting.

Three-Year Curriculum

Formation roadmap

Year 1 — Biblical & Personal Foundations

Whole Bible, hermeneutics, doctrine, prayer, short expositions, sermon analysis, Holy Spirit theology.

Year 2 — Pastoral Competence

Shepherding, full sermons, eldership, church doctrine, administration, finances, conflict, safeguarding.

Year 3 — Leadership & Multiplication

Develop leaders, advanced preaching, church planting, community exegesis, and external readiness assessment.
